04 / NOVELTY · PROBLEM

A small league cannot train a good win predictor on its own

The PSL has played {{ leagueTotal('PSL', 'Matches') }} decided matches since 2016, against {{ leagueTotal('IPL', 'Matches') }} IPL matches in the paper's dataset alone. Trained on PSL data only, the paper's method is less accurate and its probabilities are unreliable: for the boosting models its Brier score is close to or worse than 0.25, the score of always predicting 50 %. The paper's raw features (runs of batsman 1–11 and bowler 1–10) also never state what really decides a chase: how many runs are needed, how fast, and with how many wickets and balls left.

  1. Chase-state features (7): runs needed, current and required run rate, their gap, resources (wickets left × balls left) and momentum over the last 12 balls.
  2. League-aware transfer: train on IPL + earlier PSL seasons together, with a league flag.
  3. Honest evaluation: six PSL seasons (2021–2026), each predicted only from earlier seasons, with probability quality (Brier) reported as well as the five metrics.
